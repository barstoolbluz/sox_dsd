## Decisions and corrections

**No narrowing of the gate.** All 30 integer-family and 24 fractional-family cells remain mandatory; even an integer destination can require the actual final interpolation stage. Independent mathematical/implementation routes, not distinct human authorship, determine the reference's eligibility. The existing 75-decimal-digit bank-composed FIR and independently constructed rational polyphase SRC are retained. The differently designed SRC's finite-file discrepancies remain **measurements**, not fictional roundoff failures or arbitrary blanket tolerances. The frozen `analysis-v1` is not substituted for decoder metrology.

A **demonstrated evidence-custody defect** was fixed: removing `session.json` after a completed row previously allowed `--resume` to create a new session descriptor and reuse old results. The revised runner rejects a lost session or worklist, binds the worklist/reference dependencies, checks every required raw/SRC/graph receipt, and refuses changed tier coverage. Raw ISA program, isolated FIR and selected impulse samples are now saved in the per-cell NPZ for independent replay. The scalar independent-reference phase alignment is now **asserted**, not merely printed as a value.

New **optional raw graph capture** persists the genuine six-channel DSF, both graph-produced int32 WAVs and the clip-warning WAV with relative SHA-256 receipts. The native chronology and matched six-channel F64 oracle are in the same NPZ; channel 5 intentionally duplicates channel 0 for a second channel-isolation assertion. Independent review recomputes int32 WAV differences from the F64 samples and verifies the allowed one-LSB graph variance. With `--capture-src-raw`, the review recomputes sampled tone fits from original F64 output instead of trusting typed spectral numbers. Raw output filenames and receipt contents contain no machine-specific absolute path. The original per-stream clip-error behavioral assertion still runs in the instrument; a failed command has no artifact to archive.

`--require-all-observations` refuses short/filtered runs and missing SRC/SoX probes, but **never** claims an all-extrema certificate or a physical-host acceptance. The new `p15_matrix_receipt_review.py` checks separate source-compatible ISA hosts and counts 54 scalar, 216 additional functional forced tiers (270 including scalar), and 96 predeclared detailed comparisons. An unsupported tier remains missing; a failing observation cannot be suppressed by a passing copy from another host. It independently verifies stored vectors/digests and records which cells actually retain raw SRC/graph files. It does not claim cryptographic attestation from untrusted SHA sidecars.

Changed existing files: `tests/dsd/p15_matrix_instrument.py`, `tests/dsd/p15_graph_integration.py`, `tests/dsd/test_p15_instrument.py`, `docs/dsd/P15_MATRIX_INSTRUMENT.md`, and the self-excluding root index (regenerated last). New files: `tests/dsd/p15_matrix_receipt_review.py` and this checkpoint. **No production DSP, ABI, FIR coefficient bank, profile limit, default switch, or historical result file was changed.**
