### SRC envelope ruling

A **universal per-sample maximum error against a different-design independent
Kaiser SRC is out of scope**: two legitimate resamplers can give different
samples for the same source while both meet their transfer specifications.
The 1e-5 to 1e-3 boundary/interior observations do not establish a defect
by themselves; importing the FIR-only section 8.6 accumulated-roundoff bound
for an SRC with a different transfer is invalid.

The **production composite SRC spectral envelope remains mandatory and is
already specified**, not newly invented: section 6.1's 0.0001-dB passband and
-160-dB full stopband/coherent alias constraints, evaluated for the effective
representable passband and actual complete pipeline. Section 10.4 also calls
for independently measured SRC arithmetic agreement. To do that comparison,
implement the **same** production transfer/phase/finite-file zero-extension
convention independently with high-precision coefficients or an equivalent
bounded operator, and declare its error accounting **before** comparison.
The already-retained different-filter reference remains an excellent transfer
cross-check, but cannot be used as a like-for-like sample oracle. No tolerance
is relaxed and no redesign is ordered by this review.

### Intel amendment: explicit decision and future hinge

The source specification required physical Intel AVX-512 evidence; earlier
section 13 exceptions did not encompass it. The operator requested release
scope reduction because this representative host is unavailable, not a
substitute test. That request is **approved only for the specific physical
performance/default-preference claim**, with its operative exact wording in
`DSD_DESIGN.md` section 10.7 and rationale in
`docs/dsd/amendments/P15_INTEL_AVX512_SCOPE_2026-10-14.md`.

An unknown CPU still selects SSE2 when supported, otherwise scalar. Higher
forceable ISA tiers continue to require correct CPU/OS feature gating.
**No** Intel-specific automatic AVX-512 selection, physical-performance
ranking, AMD/Intel performance equivalence, physical Intel compatibility
certification or default promotion is claimed. The original Intel test must
be recorded **UNRUN**, visible alongside the scope exception. Before any
future release makes one of these claims or enables such preference, restore
its physical Intel hardware requirement and review the corresponding evidence.
This is neither a general Intel exception nor a waiver of numerical,
correctness, stability or benchmark gates.

### DSD256: smallest corrective experiment

Do **not** rerun P15 wholesale or erase the R2 measurements. Prepare one
staged legacy control whose independently measured transfer matches the
**actual** DSD256 mastering profile (40-kHz guaranteed passband, 80-kHz
stopband transition and appropriate 176.4/352.8-kHz output behavior), rather
than a control whose effective passband is only 20 kHz. Verify its passband
and pre-/post-decimation alias behavior with high-frequency holdout stimuli;
R2's single low-frequency input cannot establish that equality. Then measure
at least 15 repeated timings on the same controlled host/fixture/build
under that control and retain all commands, actual output-rate checks,
individual samples, clock/affinity evidence and CI method. Independently
recompute the new result before changing gate status. The R2 direct-path
speedups already stand as a **bounded direct-reference observation**; they
are not discarded or relabeled as a matched-bandwidth test.
