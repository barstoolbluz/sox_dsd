- **Measure `dff-6ch-repack-small` specifically, and watch the running total.**
  It has been the worst workload for three phases and has needed a hotfix
  twice. Its cumulative movement from P05 through P08, computed from the
  recorded median ratios, is +3.20%: each phase passed its own 5% gate, but the
  design gates per phase and not the accumulation, so that total is now closer
  to the limit than any single delta.
- The gate is 5% median regression, admissible only when noise is under 2%, and
  the verdict comes from the confidence interval rather than the median. A
  straddling interval is inconclusive, not a pass. P08's round applied this
  correctly to refuse a claim its +0.69% median would have supported;
  `tests/dsd/BENCHMARKING.md` and `tests/dsd/benchprep.sh` cover method and
  host preparation.
- `tests/dsd/bench_read_p02.py` has four options worth knowing:
  `--null-extra-arg` for `-t null` runs, `--both-arg` for an option both arms
  need, `--operation` to select a workload type, and `--require-comparable`
  with its `p01p02` preset for a hotfix that must not change what it decodes.
  The first probes both binaries and records unavailable workloads rather than
  counting them as errors.
