# Quarantined exploratory third-pass experiments

These are authentic observations from temporary, rigorous but **not delivered** adaptive Taylor experiments. They are excluded from the final benchmarks and delivered mathematical certificate claims because they cannot be replayed byte-for-byte with the frozen source. The original receipts are retained for provenance rather than deleted. `new_campaign_receipts/` originated in this exploratory period.

The adaptive-order approach was discarded after its end-to-end cost/quality regression. The final source uses unchanged second-pass Taylor evaluation combined with the source-exact finite-difference upper bound.
